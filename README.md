## What I'm building

### <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=swift&amp;theme=light" width="23" height="23" alt="Swift logo" /> [Kinetic](https://github.com/harelmtr-sketch/kinetic-showcase)
**Co-founder & CTO · Live on the App Store**

Kinetic helps people discover calisthenics skills, understand their prerequisites, and track progression from their first pull-up to advanced movements.

**What I built:**

- Proposed and carried out the native iOS rebuild after our initial React Native version, using SwiftUI for the app and SpriteKit for its interactive skill tree.
- Built the progression system that turns logged reps and hold times into ranks, tracks progress toward the next threshold, and unlocks skills when prerequisites are met.
- Built and integrated accounts, saved progress, subscription access, purchase restoration, and product analytics.

I co-founded Kinetic with **[Ilay Gibor](https://github.com/ilaygibor)**, who leads calisthenics content and growth and also contributes to product and engineering. Our goal is to make Kinetic the go-to platform for learning and progressing in calisthenics.

**Built with:** Swift, SwiftUI, SpriteKit, Supabase, RevenueCat, and PostHog. The production source code is private.

**[View the project showcase](https://github.com/harelmtr-sketch/kinetic-showcase) · [Try Kinetic on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kinetic-calisthenics-training/id6766755091)**

### <img src="https://cdn.simpleicons.org/apple/8B7CF6" width="23" height="23" alt="Apple WidgetKit icon" /> [GymPal](https://github.com/harelmtr-sketch/Gympal-showcase)
**Native iOS · In development**

A mascot-led workout accountability app built with SwiftUI and WidgetKit. It keeps planned workouts visible through iPhone widgets, with an expressive coach, a native gym map, and a location-aware check-in.

**What I built:** The character-led interface and animations, Apple Maps gym discovery, photo and proximity check-in, and app/widget accountability interactions. Built collaboratively with [Ilay Gibor](https://github.com/ilaygibor).

**Built with:** Swift, SwiftUI, WidgetKit, App Intents, MapKit, and Supabase. The production source remains private.

**[View the GymPal showcase](https://github.com/harelmtr-sketch/Gympal-showcase)**
